Kruger Body & Paint

A website concept for a family-owned panel beater in Booysens, Johannesburg, styled like a workshop spec sheet.

The idea

Panel beaters are hard to trust from the outside. This concept wins trust with precision: real measurements, tolerances and processes instead of vague promises.

The industrial type and condensed caps borrow from workshop signage and job cards.

What stands out

  • A “bay status” board in the hero showing bench availability, booth cure cycle, cycle time and courtesy cars.
  • Every repair type listed with its technical spec, such as frame tolerance and clear-coat thickness.
  • Estimate requests, a paint-code check and 24-hour towing placed up front.
  • Heritage and accreditation shown as facts: family owned since 1984, accredited shop, qualified artisans.
